## Overview

This project analyzes customer churn patterns within a telecom company using SQL, Python, Excel, and Power BI.

The objective is to identify customer segments with high churn rates, understand key churn drivers, and provide data-driven retention recommendations.

## Key Findings

- Month-to-Month customers exhibited the highest churn levels.
- Customers within their first 12 months were most likely to leave.
- Fiber Optic customers accounted for the highest churn volume.
- Higher monthly charges were associated with increased churn.
- Contract type emerged as the strongest predictor of churn.

---

## Business Recommendations

1. Encourage migration from Month-to-Month contracts to annual plans.
2. Improve onboarding programs for new customers.
3. Investigate churn among Fiber Optic subscribers.
4. Develop loyalty programs for high-value customers.
5. Implement targeted retention campaigns for high-risk segments.
