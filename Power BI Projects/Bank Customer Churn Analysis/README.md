# Bank Customer Churn Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn behaviour within a retail banking environment to identify key churn drivers and improve customer retention.

## Key Findings

- Churn Rate: 20.37%
- Germany recorded the highest churn levels.
- Inactive customers churned significantly more than active customers.
- Customers aged 31–60 accounted for the majority of churn.
- Customers with Fair and Poor credit scores exhibited higher churn levels.
- Customers holding only one product represented the largest churn segment.

## Business Recommendations

1. Develop targeted retention campaigns for inactive customers.
2. Strengthen customer engagement among middle-aged customer groups.
3. Increase product cross-selling to reduce single-product dependency.
4. Improve customer retention initiatives within Germany.
5. Create loyalty programs for customers with lower credit quality profiles.
